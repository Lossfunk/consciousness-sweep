# Methods for an auditable sweep of awareness and downstream mental function

This document is a human-readable rendering of the current prospective repair. It is derived, not normative: `PROTOCOL.md` governs scientific decisions and `AGENTS.md` governs execution. If wording here conflicts with either authority, the authority controls. Protocol/package 0.9.14 and schema 0.9.5 identify this successor; its release-readiness record determines launch authorization. Sweep launch remains a separate action.

The retained scientific criteria evaluate combined result-bearing awareness evidence and reasonable transfer without requiring exact procedure identity, trialwise reports or perfect higher-regime seeing. It distinguishes signed signal amplification from a diagnostic same-function pattern and binds the latter to its actual control. Statistical concerns are assessed by their effect on the scoped inference; a sensitivity analysis crossing .05 alone is not a veto, and qualified descriptive preservation can retain nonfatal concerns in both positive-capacity and direct-nondifference analyses. Actual awareness support, positive whole-function evidence, direct tests and explicit source links remain required under the protocol. Current readiness is reported by its input-bound receipts. Repair validation covers software, synthetic behavior and execution boundaries; it is not a new empirical panel or a measurement of recall.

<a id="methods-01"></a>

The empirical target is human consciousness, including directly informative patient studies. Other-system evidence requires an explicit human link and remains supplementary. Awareness contrasts may be aware/unaware, graded or probabilistic; an unaware arm is not compulsory. Exact-content awareness and comparison validity are audited separately from signal control. Opposing instructions confounded with awareness do not constitute a comparable contrast; properly crossed designs can. A common endogenous processing transition is compatible with the association aim.

Main findings require a comparable human awareness contrast. Claims of an awareness-linked difference or positive invariance require strong A/E/F; a positive unaware-capacity claim within that comparative design requires strong A/F, without pretending that weak E establishes an awareness effect. Capacity-only findings remain supplementary and visible; supported whole-Y unaware capacity can still trigger a bounded function search without strong E. All screen-in, screen-uncertain and title-retained abstract-unavailable candidates receive full-text attempts. Strong A/F does not force a capacity assessment when no unaware condition was tested.

Interpretable results with nonfatal statistical concerns remain qualified evidence. Reviewer-derived calculations and compatible directly tested joint analyses require explicit reproducible provenance and transfer justification. Bayesian model-relative null support (`null-supported`) is distinct from equivalence within a justified region. Graded null/equivalence findings do not require an unaware arm; neither establishes preserved capacity without separate positive evidence. Meyen is checked and displayed separately, with no automatic eligibility or main-ranking penalty. Evidence ordering is qualitative with reasons; conflicting evidence remains a scoped mixed synthesis, and suggestive/complementary findings remain visible.

The frozen blind lane has fourteen QREV6 coverage families on two sources, one ranked N≤40 window each (at most 1,120 encounters). Pain/unpleasantness, pleasure/liking and motivation/reward have separate windows. The coverage inventory and its rationale are in queries.md; it is not an eligibility whitelist. Exact revised strings are prospectively specified and locally checked; live retrieval quality remains a commissioning target. Documented same-function terminology from an identified source may enter QDYN with an identity rationale. Freezing these windows does not start a sweep.

## 1. Aim and scientific question

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §1; AGENTS §1.

The sweep asks: for an information-bearing content or entity **X**, whether external or internally generated, and a downstream mental function **Y**, does awareness of X change whether, or how well, Y can use X? It is intentionally narrower than a review of all neural or behavioural correlates of consciousness. An eligible comparison must connect X to an auditable downstream use Y and provide an awareness contrast or a justified attempt to establish one.

The practical deliverable is a source-grounded prospect queue for human vetting. The contribution, decisive evidence, remaining alternative and specific human question precede any tentative primary/support/undetermined role. A plausible awareness–use comparison may remain scientifically unresolved; known absence of that relation remains context. The prospect queue does not weaken the scientific criteria.

The method is result-neutral. A comparison may support processing without awareness, an awareness-linked difference, no detectable difference, equivalence within a stated region, or an unresolved conclusion. Search, screening, and comparison formation cannot depend on which result is expected or preferred.

Y may be any mental function, including affective, motivational, experiential, social, perceptual, and conventionally cognitive functions. A behavioral or physiological outcome can support such a function when justified. The supplementary search families are not an eligibility whitelist. There is no publication-date exclusion; preprints and later available full-text versions are eligible, with exact versions and retrieval times retained.

The target is a well-supported co-occurrence between awareness and changed downstream use that survives the relevant simple explanations, including first-order signal strength. These associations constrain the next abductive step of constructing a process architecture. Independent causal attribution to awareness is not required: awareness and the functional change may accompany a common processing transition. The existing A/E/F and statistical criteria determine support; this aim adds no separate effect-size or causal-identification threshold.

The atomic scientific record is one **paper × physical experiment × X→Y comparison**. A paper-level label is too coarse when a paper contains several experiments or functions; an effect-level label is too fine when several components jointly express one coherent functional relation.

<a id="methods-02"></a>
## 2. Core concepts

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §§2–5; AGENTS §§7, 10.

Three design dimensions are assessed before scientific outcomes are interpreted.

- **X** is the specific information-bearing content or entity whose awareness varies.
- **Y** is the most conservative downstream mental function that uses X, not merely a measure, effect, or proposed mechanism.
- **A: awareness evidence for X.** `A-direct` uses qualifying evidence in the focal experiment. `A-validated` uses a sufficiently transferable validation. `A-transfer-risk` records material transfer, sensitivity, or applicability uncertainty. `A-absent` means qualifying evidence is absent. A procedure is not a result; strong A needs a located, supportive critical result. Subjective evidence, informative objective invisibility evidence, or both may qualify.
- **E: first-order evidence about X.** `E-matched` requires achieved relevant performance matching by design, defensible post-hoc matching, or successful equivalence within a stated margin. `E-diagnostic` is a separate strong route for an unmatched design whose same-Y pattern, assessed under the frozen review criterion, rules out the relevant simple evidence-strength account. `E-physical-only` records nominal physical identity without adequate internal-evidence control; `E-uncontrolled` records weaker cases.
- **F: meaningful downstream use Y.** `F-meaningful` requires an auditable downstream function or coherent input→output mapping. `F-ambiguous` retains a plausible downstream use that cannot be assigned conservatively. `F-not-meaningful` covers direct registration, a generic neural response, or an effect label that does not establish a downstream function. Mechanistic uncertainty alone does not make F ambiguous.

Two inference axes remain independent. **Unaware capacity** asks whether the exact focal Y is supported in the unaware condition; E is not its gate. **Awareness effect** asks whether aware and unaware conditions differ on the same Y; the strongest route requires strong A, strong E, meaningful F, and an adequate direct focal test.

Here, “awareness effect” names an awareness-linked comparison. A constraint-ready difference supplies a qualified association for theory construction. Its summary identifies the downstream change and the measured performance difference or specified simple account addressed by E. Matching and diagnostic control remain distinguishable; neither requires ruling out every evidence-dependent architecture, and a possible common processing transition alone is not a veto. Independently anchored alternatives still receive the protocol's bounded audit.

Region-based equivalence and Bayesian model-relative null support are distinct outcomes. Both require an interpretable direct focal or compatible joint test with explicit provenance. Neither follows from separate significance labels or a bare nonsignificant result.

<a id="methods-03"></a>
## 3. Scientific algorithm

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §§1–9; AGENTS §§6–11.

```text
discover and deduplicate logical papers
screen titles and abstracts with paper-specific semantic decisions
obtain full text or record a bounded missing-text request
for each physical experiment:
    create its durable experiment-group record
    state the potential contribution, decisive source evidence and unresolved question
    define X and Y; assess scalar versus factorial functional structure
    for a scientific promotion, obtain its required independent source reading before labels
    assess F, A, and E from design evidence before outcome inspection
    build direct evidence bundles for every borrowed premise
    evaluate unaware capacity and awareness effect independently
    audit statistics, null evidence, and Meyen where applicable
write each decision and reason continuously
derive outputs and validate each checkpoint
author/update the prospect queue, retaining prior items or their reasoned disposition
continue bounded discovery until all required and reopened frontiers close
```

The natural reading order is therefore discovery → screening → full text → physical experiment → X/Y and functional structure → F → A → E → independent outcomes → statistical and diagnostic audit. The durable writing order adds one constraint: parent records and append-only decision events are written before dependent current-state changes.

<a id="methods-04"></a>
## 4. Units of representation

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §§1, 2.5–2.6; AGENTS §§2, 5, 7.

Four objects prevent accidental merging or fragmentation.

- A **logical paper** is one deduplicated bibliographic work. A preprint and publication are linked only when authorship, content, and version relationship justify treating them as one work.
- A **physical experiment group** is one experiment as conducted and reported. Every full-text-considered experiment receives a durable row before any comparison ID is created, including support-only and no-qualifying-comparison experiments.
- A **comparison** is one conservative X→Y mapping in one experiment. `comparison_id` is the unique scientific row identifier; an `experiment_id` may recur across several comparison rows.
- A **support-only experiment** supplies evidence for another comparison but does not independently answer an eligible X→Y question. It receives an experiment-group record and direct evidence links, never a placeholder comparison.

The current inventory counts only active physical groups. A later source-backed finding that an allocated group is a procedure part, reanalysis or duplicate bookkeeping entry retires that nonphysical record without deleting its ID or history. Its explicit reason and optional current same-paper reconciliation target remain stored. Current comparison, joint-analysis, evidence and recommendation references cannot use a retired record; structured references must be reconciled in the same locked transaction. This does not retire real experiments with uninformative results or automatically transfer scientific judgments. Historical runs retain their original schemas.

Stable identifiers use the registered forms `P000001`, `P000001_E01`, `P000001_E01_C01`, `<RUN_ID>_D000001`, `<RUN_ID>_S000001`, `<RUN_ID>_R000001`, `<RUN_ID>_AUX000001`, `<RUN_ID>_EL000001`, `<RUN_ID>_EB000001`, `<RUN_ID>_EA000001`, and `<RUN_ID>_FT000001`. They are allocated once through the locked writer and never recycled. Allocation advances durable state before an ID is returned, so a crash may retire an unused ID but cannot permit reuse. Resume reconciles every next counter against durable records.

<a id="methods-05"></a>
## 5. Discovery strategy

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §§9–9.4; AGENTS §§8, 11–12.

Discovery combines four routes under a principal-first, blind-last state machine.

### 5.1 Seeds and review anchors

Every reference in the current manuscript is an initial seed, placed once in the empirical candidate or review/discovery-anchor file. Theory, methods, computational works, and books are fixed discovery anchors, with no expected scientific classification. Existing supplementary anchors are retained.

Fixed empirical seeds initialize the candidate graph. Fixed reviews and other discovery anchors supply bibliographies and forward-citation routes; they are discovery infrastructure, not automatically candidate evidence. Each result encountered through any event is retained, including rediscoveries of an existing logical paper.

### 5.2 Citation expansion and one-hop bridges

Eligible CORE papers are expanded through references and citing papers in frozen waves. CORE is a discovery permission: at least one physical experiment contains a specific X, a genuine awareness-level/regime contrast or substantive attempt to assess that contrast or unaware capacity concerning X, and a distinct plausible Y coded `F-meaningful` or `F-ambiguous`. Weak final A or E does not prevent recursion. A paper limited to generic registration or priming coded `F-not-meaningful` does not automatically recurse. An unavailable `ask-text-high` paper may serve provisionally only when its abstract explicitly supports the same X + awareness contrast + distinct plausible Y structure. CORE status is not a final A/E/F code or result classification.

A strict one-hop empirical bridge (`K=1`) recovers near-neighbours. The paper must be adequately full-text-evaluated, empirical, and non-CORE, and one physical experiment must contain exactly two CORE ingredients while explicitly failing the third; unclear, unknown, or unassessed does not count as failure. Ingredients cannot be assembled across experiments, and a positive near-neighbour rationale is required. Nonempirical reviews, theories, perspectives, commentaries, methods-only works, and generic conceptual papers are excluded. A qualifying bridge encountered on a depth-0 lineage receives one backward and one forward citation window at depth 1. Another bridge cannot continue that lineage; a genuine CORE child resets depth to 0. A bridge first seen at depth 1 becomes expandable if later reached independently at depth 0, so discovery order cannot permanently suppress its eligible hop. Identical completed paper/source/windows are reused while every encounter retains provenance. Bridge status affects discovery only: it neither establishes eligibility nor activates function-targeted search, and its windows inherit ordinary citation sources, fallbacks, and bounds. There is no paper, API, title, or parent quota, and zero yield is not a special stopping rule.

### 5.3 Function-targeted search

A qualifying comparable awareness contrast or a supported supplementary whole-Y unaware capacity earns bounded QDYN search. E does not gate the capacity route. Each source supplies one N≤50 window per normalized Y, no second page or quota redistribution. Canonical terms, mechanical variants, source-paper terminology and documented same-function external terminology require provenance and rationale. No neighbouring-function expansion is permitted. Screen-in and screen-uncertain candidates receive full-text attempts.

### 5.4 Blind-spot search

After provisional satisfaction of the principal frontiers, all fourteen prepared blind families run on both sources, with one ranked window of at most 40 records per family/source. Screen every return, retain service hit counts and truncation, and request no second page or quota redistribution. A full ranked window is not source exhaustion. New independently eligible nodes and earned functions reopen the existing frontiers under unchanged gates.

### 5.5 Reopening and stopping

Blind-last describes order, not optionality. A blind-discovered CORE node reopens citation expansion, and a genuinely new qualifying Y opens or reopens function-targeted search. Ordinary citation expansion stops only when a complete backward-and-forward wave yields no new potentially relevant paper after deduplication and screening. Completion requires every principal frontier, bridge obligation, blind window, and reopened frontier to satisfy its unchanged stopping rule.

<a id="methods-06"></a>
## 6. Search execution, snapshots, and provenance

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §§9–9.4; AGENTS §§6, 8, 11.

Each query or citation action is a search event linked to its route, wave, source, trigger, parameters, and reason. Every encountered result receives its own discovery row, even when deduplication resolves it to a known paper. Process events link to the comparison that activated them; bridge events record parent, ingredient pattern, lineage, and depth.

Every independent or function-targeted query window is an immutable retrieval snapshot identified by phase, source, family or normalized Y, canonical rendered query, source parameters, publication cutoff, requested N, and a deterministic window key. A completed snapshot records retrieval time and the ordered result-set hash. Resume reuses it only when the complete key and durable ordered discoveries agree. It cannot silently rerun, extend, rerank, substitute, or take a second page. Frozen source blocks also specify ranking, page size, screening scope, ties, reordering, cursor/API truncation, and cross-block deduplication. A failed, partial, or inconsistent snapshot remains incomplete until explicitly resolved.

OpenAlex is the primary citation source. If a parent or direction is unavailable, try Europe PMC where it covers the parent; for backward references only, then try Crossref and the article/repository reference list. No automatic citation union is required. Mandatory OpenAlex and Europe PMC query windows cannot replace one another. Queries specify complete citation pagination, retries, ties, and unavailable-source handling; missing coverage stays incomplete rather than becoming an empty result. No date filter is applied: the explicit unrestricted policy stores a null cutoff in each snapshot.

<a id="methods-07"></a>
## 7. Bibliographic identity and screening

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §§6–6.2; AGENTS §§8, 9.1–9.2.

DOIs are percent-decoded, stripped of resolver or `doi:` prefixes, trimmed, and lowercased before comparison. Identical non-empty normalized DOIs normally identify one work unless contrary evidence is recorded. Without a DOI, normalized title, year, and consistently normalized author strings are compared without guessing author identity. Uncertain matches remain separate as possible duplicates. Each confirmed merge keeps all discovery history, points the duplicate to the retained paper, and records a reason and decision event. Before bulk persistence, a non-mutating DOI/URL normalization dry run must pass for the complete proposed batch; individual rows still undergo schema validation.

Title and abstract screening are conservative, semantic, and paper-specific. Title batches contain at most 25 works and abstract batches at most 10; batching only amortizes context. Every logical paper receives an individual decision and reason. Regexes, keywords, embeddings, citation counts, numerical relevance scores, and hidden lexical heuristics cannot independently produce exclusion. Prior decisions can be reused only for the same logical work, unchanged decision-relevant evidence, and the exact same scientific-regime fingerprint.

Title screening asks whether the report could contain an empirical awareness contrast or attempt involving X and a distinct downstream Y. Abstract screening then assesses empirical relevance and assigns full-text priority without pretending to make final comparison classifications. If an abstract cannot reasonably be obtained, the paper is marked `abstract_status = unavailable`, remains `not-screened` at abstract level with a reason, and may proceed to retrieval on title and provenance. Missing abstract never becomes screen-out by itself.

<a id="methods-08"></a>
## 8. Full-text acquisition and uncertainty

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §§7–7.1; AGENTS §§9, 9.6.

Final comparison classification requires full text. Retrieval proceeds in order from published full text, to an open repository or accepted manuscript, to a permitted preprint containing the experiment; user-supplied files can resolve recorded requests. Unresolved requests are classified `ask-text-high`, `ask-text-medium`, or `unavailable-low-priority`, then ordered by protocol priority and stable paper ID. At any checkpoint, at most `ask_text_batch_size` (currently 10) are surfaced. Independent work continues while texts are missing.

Each obtained text is stored or referenced under the run's `fulltexts/` directory and recorded with its paper/version identity, repository-relative path, hash where practical, acquisition route, actual acquisition time and wave, and whether it was obtained by the agent, user, or genuinely preexisted the invocation. Availability is never backdated. A missing-text transition is logged before current state changes. Newly supplied text is evaluated on resume and can reopen citation or function-targeted stopping conditions.

One verified immutable full-text asset represents one logical article version. An unchanged version and extraction may be reused across comparisons when file hash, extraction tool/version, extracted-text hash, and page map agree. A materially different article version receives a new asset; no version or current-state change is overwritten silently.

Unavailable or classification-critical information is represented explicitly. If an auxiliary source cannot be obtained, its dependent conclusion remains unresolved; the reviewer does not infer the missing result. Uncertainty records what is missing, why it matters, the attempted resolution, and which route or field remains blocked.

<a id="methods-09"></a>
## 9. Physical experiments, X, Y, and functional structure

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §§1, 2.4–2.6; AGENTS §7.

At full text, every physical experiment is enumerated first and assigned a durable experiment-group row. Before comparison IDs are created, the reviewer performs a pre-atomization functional-structure and operational-identity assessment based on the actual input→output mapping—not effect names, directions, mechanisms, or desired conclusions.

**X** is the manipulated or varying information-bearing content used in the focal relation. **Y** is the most conservative auditable downstream function supported by the dependent measure. A measure name, task label, neural signal, effect, or proposed mechanism is not automatically a function. Y must state what the system does with X.

A scalar comparison contains one coherent X→Y relation. A factorial-functional comparison retains components together when their structured pattern or interaction is essential to one conservative functional mapping. Components split when they are distinct outputs, stages, or relations. When the inference requires a factorial focal relation, that focal comparison is created before any optional component rows, and components link back to it. A component can support a separate capacity claim only through its own linked comparison. Support-only and no-qualifying groups retain their experiment row without invented comparisons.

Each completed comparison records a non-temporary functional-structure assessment, identity basis, and reason; a generic “structural audit complete” flag cannot substitute for this judgment.

Existing descriptions, reasons, and notes identify the measured outcome and the inference from that measure to Y, with relevant population, task, duration, and report limitations. Keep the information-bearing content separate from an experimenter's parameter or relation code. The same measured outcome need not identify the same function when different substantive downstream uses can produce it. Profile shape, interference or a significant interaction cannot supply the functional name without that operational argument. Missing information is explicit. Evidence for a brief capacity does not establish sustained use, and delayed access does not itself establish continuous active maintenance; no additional metadata taxonomy is required.

Before proposed A/F labels, reading tiers or expected human decisions are disclosed, the source-focused checker receives neutral source identifiers, hashes and experiment locations and durably records an independent interpretation of awareness-measured content and the dependent measure's most conservative task-level function. Existing reasons explain the readout-to-function inference and actual critical analysis, including its sample, timing and reporting scope. A conscious condition elsewhere or a later discrimination subgroup does not establish that functional comparison; a real broader comparison remains at its own scope. These source-grounded notes stay distinct from manipulation detail and proposed mechanism, and supply the later recommendation rather than a second scientific table. The existing decision log stores a source/version/hash/regime receipt, then recorded label disclosure, then a bound review. The validator checks that sequence and receipt coverage of decisive sources. It verifies recorded provenance, not hidden label access, comprehension or scientific truth; agreement with a prior verdict is not itself correctness and no scientific threshold is added.

<a id="methods-10"></a>
## 10. Functional meaning (F)

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §2.4; AGENTS §§7, 10.

F is assessed from the operational mapping. `F-meaningful` requires a downstream function or coherent factorial mapping: Y must do something with X beyond merely registering X. Direct detection or report of X, an undifferentiated neural response, and an isolated effect label are insufficient. A response-selection, updating, binding, monitoring, memory, or control claim qualifies only when the design actually supports that named use. Mechanistic interpretation may remain uncertain while functional meaning is clear.

For source-focused checking, durably state the most conservative task-level function justified by the located dependent measure before the assessor's F code is disclosed. Keep that function separate from cognitive or neural implementation. Under `PROTOCOL.md` §2.4, unresolved mechanism does not turn a clear conservative function into `F-ambiguous`; the review adds neither a requirement to prove awareness is an independent cause nor a new causal-mechanism test beyond the existing operational X→Y relation. `F-ambiguous` remains appropriate only when the function itself cannot be assigned conservatively under the existing rule.

`F-ambiguous` is used when downstream use is plausible but the function cannot be stated conservatively from the evidence. `F-not-meaningful` is used when the dependent measure does not establish a downstream function. Every code includes a source-grounded reason.

<a id="methods-11"></a>
## 11. Awareness evidence (A)

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §2.1; AGENTS §§7, 9.4.

A is specific to the focal X, condition, population, and experiment. Subjective measures can directly support awareness status. Objective invisibility evidence can also qualify, but chance-level performance is informative only if the awareness test is sensitive, appropriately administered, and statistically capable of detecting relevant information.

The record separates the awareness procedure, the reported critical result, and whether that result supports the code. Merely describing a test cannot yield strong A. `A-direct` requires a located supportive result in the focal experiment. `A-validated` requires a complete result-bearing transfer bundle. Transfer addresses stimulus parameters, masking or suppression, timing, attentional allocation, task demands, participant population, awareness measurement, first-order-evidence regime, analysis, and article/version compatibility wherever relevant; procedure-only support is incomplete. Material uncertainty yields `A-transfer-risk`, and absent qualifying evidence yields `A-absent`.

Before A is assigned, briefly transcribe the decisive awareness item or instruction and critical result, then state separately the content that item/result measures and the focal X. Manipulation detail can define the focal X but cannot upgrade the measured content beyond the awareness wording and result. If specificity remains unresolved, retain the applicable existing A category and narrower or supplementary support rather than forcing a strong label or excluding the paper.

Awareness-result reporting remains visible even when it does not support strong A. Evidence is not copied between experiments: every borrowed premise is linked directly through its focal comparison's bundle.

<a id="methods-12"></a>
## 12. First-order evidence (E)

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §§2.2–2.3; AGENTS §§7, 9.4.

E concerns task-relevant first-order information about X, not physical stimulus identity alone. `E-matched` requires achieved relevant performance matching by design, defensible post-hoc matching, or an adequate equivalence result under a stated margin. Matching on an irrelevant measure, or observing similar point estimates without an adequate test, is insufficient.

E is assessed in a design-first, outcome-neutral order. The reviewer first checks matched evidence. If that fails, `E-diagnostic` is assessed using the frozen review criterion: the observed same-Y pattern must violate a task-justified simple evidence-strength prediction. The source study need not have preregistered its result; exploratory status remains part of the statistical audit. Crossing chance alone can be ordinary improvement with stronger information and does not automatically qualify. The record states the competing account, its prediction, the observed violation, and the design feature that makes the contrast diagnostic. In a factorial-functional case, a direct member must address the stated first-order-evidence alternative; an interaction alone cannot complete the bundle.

Every unmatched case undergoes a bounded whole-article alternative-control scan before finalizing E-diagnostic, E-physical-only, or E-uncontrolled, so a relevant control is not missed merely because the initial design looks weak. A viable unresolved alternative anchored in an actual task-state contrast blocks the code. Unanchored speculation about latent states cannot gate E. The final record identifies the contrast-integrity disposition and its direct evidential or design anchor. Cases without strong control remain `E-physical-only` or `E-uncontrolled` with reasons.

E affects the strength of awareness-effect inference but does not erase a direct descriptive contrast and is not a gate for unaware capacity.

<a id="methods-13"></a>
## 13. Evidence bundles, auxiliary sources, and factual reuse

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §§7.1–7.2; AGENTS §§9.3–9.5.

Any scientific premise borrowed from another experiment, analysis, supplement, or external work uses one explicit bundle for one named premise of one focal comparison. Before results are used, the bundle declares every logically required `member_key`. Each direct link names one source, supported claim, support role, dependency role (`required`, `strengthening`, `robustness`, `generalization`, or `mechanistic`), status, transfer reason, limitation, and source location. External works are recorded once as reusable auxiliary sources; consulting them does not place them in the candidate pool or make them citation nodes unless they are independently encountered through ordinary discovery.

Bundle status is derived. It is `conflicted` if any direct member contradicts or is conflicted; otherwise `incomplete` if any required member is absent, unavailable, unevaluated, or transfer-invalid; otherwise `complete` when at least one decision-used member supports the premise and all required members support it. No scientific conclusion can depend on an incomplete or conflicted bundle. Links are direct and non-transitive: sharing a paper or bundle cannot propagate a conclusion to another comparison.

Transfer is premise-specific and addresses population, manipulation, measure, analysis, and version where relevant. An awareness-A bundle must contain a required, decision-used critical-result member; a procedure description cannot complete it. An E-diagnostic bundle must contain its direct design/evidence anchors.

Execution-only evidence assessments may cache factual observations—task structure, measure identity, reported analysis, or a located result—only for the exact source/version, asset hash, premise, source location, and scientific-regime fingerprint. They cannot store or propagate A/E/F codes, eligibility, route outcomes, Meyen conclusions, or comparison-level judgments. Every focal comparison still requires its own scientific bundle and links.

<a id="methods-14"></a>
## 14. Route-aware outcome evaluation

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §§3, 8; AGENTS §7.

After X/Y, structure, F, A, E, and required bundles are resolved as far as the evidence permits, two routes are evaluated independently. The record first states the result-inspection disposition, then preserves each route's prerequisites, focal source pointers, statistics, and uncertainty. A paper or comparison is not forced into one mutually exclusive headline class.

For every comparison, the reviewer records whether the unaware condition supports the exact focal Y and whether evidence covers the whole focal Y. Separately, the reviewer records whether a direct aware/unaware test on that same Y occurred, which experiment produced it, where it appears, and what statistic or test was reported. A weak E closes the constraint-ready awareness-effect route but does not erase the direct descriptive result or independently close the capacity route.

<a id="methods-15"></a>
## 15. Unaware capacity

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §3.1; AGENTS §10.

An unaware-capacity claim exactly names the audited whole focal Y. Positive evidence under an explicitly selected validated unaware regime can support it with regime-local strong A and meaningful F and interpretable adequate or nonfatally qualified statistics; E is not required. A component effect cannot substitute for its parent mapping. Capacity-only findings remain supplementary and can earn function search. No unaware arm means not-tested, not a negative capacity claim.

This route can coexist with an awareness-linked difference: the unaware condition may support Y while awareness still changes its magnitude or form. It can also coexist with equivalence-supported invariance.

<a id="methods-16"></a>
## 16. Awareness effects and direct descriptive contrasts

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §§3.2, 4.1; AGENTS §10.

The awareness-effect categories are difference-supported, no-detectable-difference, equivalence-supported, null-supported, unresolved and not-tested. A supported association requires a comparable awareness contrast and strong A/E/F, with interpretable statistics. Record focal or joint analysis scope, contributing experiments, exact source location, test and origin. Joint analysis requires a complete transfer bundle and a directly tested same-Y contrast. Reviewer-derived statistics require located inputs and a reproducible calculation, assumptions and limitations.

Every direct same-Y aware/unaware comparison is audited even when E is weak or another strong-route requirement fails. Its descriptive result is retained without upgrading it to a constraint-ready awareness interpretation. If a performed test has an uninterpretable or insufficiently reported estimate, record that limitation and an unresolved result; a completed audit need not yield a conclusive answer. This separation prevents design weakness from erasing reported evidence while preventing a descriptive result from claiming more than the design supports.

<a id="methods-17"></a>
## 17. Null results, equivalence, statistics, and Meyen

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §§4–5; AGENTS §10.

Region-based equivalence and Bayesian model-relative null support are distinct outcomes. Both require an interpretable direct focal or compatible joint test with explicit provenance. Neither follows from separate significance labels or a bare nonsignificant result.

The Meyen role, result and assessability are recorded separately. No Meyen category automatically excludes evidence or penalizes the main ranking. A/E/F and statistical judgments require their own source-based reasons.

Every full-text comparison receives an overall statistical status of `adequate`, `concern`, or `unclear`. Each claimed result is audited at the level required by its route: focal estimate and test, uncertainty, multiple comparisons, exclusions, model specification, pseudoreplication, and exploratory status where relevant. “Significant in aware, nonsignificant in unaware” is not evidence that conditions differ, and a generic main effect or component test cannot substitute for the critical X→Y or aware/unaware contrast.

Before proposed labels are disclosed, the independent source receipt states the measured outcome, actual analysis, sample/unit, reported convention and numerical consistency. The existing statistics review retains those source observations and binds the exact focal comparison/function/critical analyses. Explicit calculations are checked using ordinary Student-t, normal-z or F reference distributions only where applicable; df and tail are reported parameters or named assumptions, never inferred silently. Bootstrap, permutation, multilevel, Bayesian, equivalence and neural conventions require their actual interpretation. The helper verifies arithmetic, not scientific eligibility. A specific reason explains a noncomputed analysis and whether any uncertainty is critical or nonfatal; confirmation is inconsistent with critical unresolved statistics. No additional scientific table or numerical significance gate is introduced.

## 18. Derived evidence sets and reporting

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §12; AGENTS §§2, 10.

Derived files are generated mechanically from validated master rows and sorted by paper, experiment, and comparison IDs. They are never classified separately. Views include awareness-effect design eligibility, constraint-ready awareness-linked differences, constraint-ready unaware capacities, equivalence-supported invariance, descriptive no-detectable differences, unresolved comparisons, Bayesian model-relative null support, supplementary evidence, and the main human comparable findings. Compatible views overlap.

Constraint-ready conclusions require fully evaluated comparisons with completed structural and result audits. The design view may retain provisional design-coded rows. Export validates the compatible master snapshot, including evidence dependencies, under the write lock before replacing views; full validation checks their exact membership, content, and order against the current master. Stale or damaged old views can be regenerated from valid masters.

The run summary reports wave and route progress, counts, unresolved cases, missing texts, validation outcomes, and remaining stopping conditions. `papers_master.csv`, `experiment_groups.csv`, and `experiments_master.csv` hold current paper, experiment, and comparison state. Search/discovery, missing-text, auxiliary-source, full-text, assessment, bundle, and link tables preserve their corresponding provenance. `decision_log.jsonl` is append-only history; `run_state.json` is the durable checkpoint.

No presentation role changes scientific export membership or discovery obligations.

At every validated checkpoint, the coordinator authors or updates the default queue, accounts for newly assessed candidates and carries earlier entries forward or records their changed disposition. A provisional guide permits open frontiers and unfinished prospect judgments. A final guide retains all original run completion requirements. Source/comparison changes require updating affected arguments; unchanged work is reused. Explicit prior human acceptances/rejections remain visible and outside the first-read queue. Only an actual human decision reopens or changes that placement. See `docs/run_quickstart.md` and `docs/postrun_handoff.md`.

## 19. Continuous audit trail and revisions

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §12; AGENTS §§5–7.

Records are written continuously, not reconstructed from memory. A parent is persisted before its ID is referenced. When correcting an error, distinguish missing/misread source evidence, claim scope and lost/stale state from genuine policy ambiguity. The former call for correction under existing rules; tentative role disagreement is not by itself a reason to change them. Every search event and encountered result, retrieval and full-text asset, missing-text state, experiment group, comparison, evidence object, deduplication, classification, statistic, uncertainty, and state transition receives a short reason. Source locations identify pages, figures, tables, analyses, assets, bundles, or links where possible; absence of a pointer is explained when material.

Current-state tables may change, but history does not. Before any revision, the agent appends a decision event stating the field, old value, new value, reason, and source location, then performs the schema-aware upsert. ## 20. Start, resume, validation, and completion

**Authority:** AGENTS §§3–4, 11–12; PROTOCOL §10.

A registered invocation supplies a filesystem-safe `RUN_ID`, `RUN_MODE = start|resume`, and available harness/model metadata. The canonical environment is created from `requirements.txt`, and registered preflight must pass before scientific work. During a registered run, `PROTOCOL.md`, `AGENTS.md`, seeds, queries, config, requirements, schemas, and tools are immutable.

Start requires a nonexistent run directory and refuses overwrite; initialization creates schema-derived headers, empty histories and derived files, the full-text directory, summary, and state. Resume requires an existing compatible run, validates it before applying current schemas, verifies package/schema versions and immutable hashes, recovers counters from durable records, and appends a resume event. It never recreates or truncates data. A completed run reopens only for newly supplied requested text or an explicitly logged continuation under the same frozen protocol, with a nonempty reason.

At each search wave, writes are flushed, outputs are derived, the run is validated, the bounded missing-text batch is prepared, summary and state are updated, and any failure is resolved before another wave. The coordinator then updates the default prospect guide, retaining earlier entries or a reasoned disposition and allowing unfinished scientific judgments under native validation. Validation checks schemas, IDs and counters, references, hashes, reasons, bundle statuses and members, evidence-cache fingerprints, full-text versions, route audits, and separation of auxiliary support from the candidate pool.

Completion requires provisional satisfaction of the principal frontier; complete, hash-consistent mandatory blind windows; satisfaction of every reopened citation and function-targeted frontier; evaluation of all retrievable and supplied texts; current missing-text state; complete non-conflicted evidence for conclusions; completed result and route audits; current derived files; reasons for unresolved cases; successful validation; and documented stopping evidence. Every candidate requiring full-text review reaches completed assessment or an explicit unresolved/missing-text disposition; a previous abstract priority is not a completed scientific verdict. Status is not set to complete before all conditions hold. Human acceptance of subsequent recommendations is not one of these completion conditions.

<a id="methods-21"></a>
## 21. Independent runs, workers, and adjudication

**Authority:** PROTOCOL §11; AGENTS §12.

Independent agents receive only the same immutable static package: frozen seed and anchor identity, normalization rules, schemas, queries, protocol, and execution instructions. They do not inspect another run or share dynamic results, screening decisions, comparison structures, A/E/F codes, evidence assessments, statistical or Meyen judgments, process-Y and saturation decisions, annotations, or outputs. If several workers operate inside one run, they receive already reserved non-overlapping units; all writes use that run's locked writer, and the coordinator reconciles durable state at checkpoints.

Only compatible completed runs may later be consolidated. Disagreements are adjudicated by returning to source evidence under the frozen protocol, retaining original judgments and the adjudication trail. Adjudication is not majority voting and cannot change the frozen protocol. An executing run never writes to the consolidated output area.

The post-adjudication functional profile is a concise source-linked table and prose recording functions, supporting comparison IDs, result types, principal limitations, and support confined to one study, patient, or paradigm. Multiple rows from one experiment are not independent replications. This summary requires no new synthesis schema, numerical evidence score, or commitment to a process theory.
