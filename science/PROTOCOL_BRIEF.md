# Sweep workflow: awareness and the use of information

> Derived reading aid, not a new authority. `PROTOCOL.md` is normative for scientific decisions and `AGENTS.md` for execution. This **0.9.13** protocol/package uses **0.9.5** schemas and is frozen; release readiness binds the validation receipts to the operative inputs. Sweep launch remains a separate action. The scientific reference is the user-designated Lossfunk-3 manuscript, interpreted through the user's explicit clarifications.

**Deliver prospects the author can vet.** For each candidate, explain what it might add to the awareness–use profile, the decisive evidence, the remaining alternative and the specific question worth reading for. Make this argument before a tentative primary/support label. Uncertain strength can remain a useful prospect; a known lack of an awareness comparison or a meaningful downstream use belongs in context. Preserve scientific uncertainty instead of forcing an acceptance or rejection.

Content-relevant seen/unseen, visibility and PAS-like reports may assess the subjective percept directly without a separate feature-identification response. Read the actual instruction, percept and claim scope; retain partial-awareness and exact-X limits. Presence, objective accuracy and condition labels do not automatically certify all features.

Evaluate the combined result-bearing awareness evidence and reasonable transfer at the claim's scope. Exact procedure identity, trialwise reports, a formal measure in every arm and 100% seeing are not universal requirements. Qualitative evidence can contribute when located and justified; do not invent reports or replace missing awareness evidence with a functional pattern.

## The scientific question

For a particular content X and a downstream function Y, **does awareness of X change whether, or how well, Y can use X?** The manuscript distinguishes the process-level use from the outcome that measures it. A measurable trace of X is not automatically a specified function.

Seek well-supported functional associations that address the relevant simple signal-strength explanation. These constrain later abductive theory construction. Independent causation by awareness is not required, and an association alone does not establish a shared mechanism.

Any mental function is eligible, including affective, motivational, experiential, social and perceptual uses. The manuscript also discusses bodily processes; physiological outcomes require a justified interpretation of the content-specific use, just as behavioural outcomes do. Human evidence, including directly informative patients, is the target. Other systems require a direct human link and remain supplementary. Graded and probabilistic awareness qualify. Preprints are eligible, and there is no publication-date exclusion.

## Core concepts

Use these plain-language questions. The letters are retained only as storage labels in existing schemas and detailed reference sections.

- **Awareness evidence** (`A_code`): What establishes awareness or unawareness of this exact content in the tested conditions? A procedure without a reported result is insufficient. Transferred validation needs a located result and justified applicability. Objective performance above chance alone does not establish awareness; informative objective invisibility evidence can support unawareness.
- **Signal-strength control** (`E_code`): Does achieved relevant first-order performance matching address poorer information about X, or does a diagnostic pattern challenge a justified simple signal–outcome prediction for this same function? Physical identity, similar means and generic statistical adjustment are insufficient. Matching and diagnostic designs constrain different explanations; neither must exclude every evidence-dependent architecture.
- **Functional interpretation** (`F_code`): What use of X does the outcome demonstrate? Specify a conservative function rather than a generic response or unsupported mechanism. Keep a coherent input–output mapping together; a component effect does not establish the whole function.

## Scientific algorithm

For each experiment, follow seven steps, with source locations and short reasons:

1. **Define the question.** Identify the information-bearing X, conservative mental function Y, measured outcome and population relevance separately. An experimental parameter is not itself the experienced content; an effect label or a theoretical mechanism is not itself an established function. Explain how the outcome supports use of X for Y. Enumerate physical experiments before splitting comparisons.
2. **Establish awareness.** Assess the exact content in **each** compared arm or graded regime, with the focal-task critical result and any separately justified transfer. Above-chance performance does not by itself establish a higher-awareness arm. Record whether an unaware condition was actually tested; lower awareness need not mean unawareness. Keep comparison-wide A for the contrast and code each regime separately. Ordinary capacity uses explicit selected exact-X unaware regimes and their positive whole-Y result; an uncertain higher arm does not block that route or its search, but remains a limit on awareness-contrast and primary-preservation claims.
3. **Check comparability.** Determine whether instructions, scoring, task requirements or measurement differences trivially produce the contrast. A properly crossed design may address this. A processing transition accompanying awareness and changed use is compatible with the aim.
4. **Assess the signal explanation.** First check achieved relevant performance matching. Otherwise state the simple account, its justified prediction, the diagnostic observation and its limits. A growing positive benefit and a growing negative cost can both be signed signal amplification; their interaction alone is not diagnostic. Bind a claim to the experiment/control that tests the alternative or an explicitly justified joint premise. Scan the article and available supplements for relevant controls before finalizing the judgment. Apply the bounded, independently anchored alternative check in the protocol; do not invent latent alternatives to veto a result.
5. **Separate the findings.** Assess the directly tested awareness comparison, whole-Y use under validated exact-X unawareness, and partial-X residual effects independently. Locate the actual critical test and its experiment, sample, timing and report order. A conscious condition elsewhere, a later discrimination subgroup or separate significance labels cannot supply that functional comparison. Preserve real broader tests at their own scope. A partial-X effect is not formal exact-X capacity. Address null or equivalence evidence only when relevant.
6. **Weigh support.** Audit the actual contrast, precision, robustness, provenance and dependence. State whether a concern invalidates the inference, weakens confidence or changes precision. Task-quality exclusions or a sensitivity analysis crossing .05 alone cannot decide eligibility. Qualified descriptive preservation may retain nonfatal concerns in both the positive capacity and direct nondifference analyses; it still needs their actual evidence and is not equivalence. Record Meyen separately without an automatic exclusion or ranking penalty.
7. **Record the argument.** State the scoped claim, decisive evidence, remaining alternative and population/task limits. For a prospect, identify what the author should resolve before adding a tentative role. Preserve suggestive, complementary and conflicting evidence without presenting it as established support.

Do not apply all special-case checks to every comparison. The table below identifies when additional records are needed. Unresolved evidence remains unresolved; completion of an audit does not require a positive conclusion.

## Four discovery routes

1. **Seeds and anchors:** the default includes all references in the current manuscript, plus retained supplementary anchors. A newly frozen commissioned scope may instead use explicit author-vetted primary/support identities; broad review anchors and the blind lane remain required. Bibliographic identity and prior author selection convey no expected scientific classification. Seed-relative distance does not establish global depth in an earlier graph.
2. **Citation expansion:** expand eligible empirical papers backward and forward, including weak evidence when the design is relevant. Strict empirical near-misses receive only the protocol's one-hop bridge expansion. A newly encountered fully eligible discovery node can resume ordinary recursion. Stop only under the fixed complete-wave criterion.
3. **Function-targeted search:** a comparable awareness contrast with qualifying awareness, signal control and functional interpretation earns a search. Independently supported whole-function use under validated unawareness can also earn one without signal matching, including supplementary capacity-only evidence. Search the same function using the canonical label, mechanical variants, source-paper terms, or documented same-function terminology from another identified source with an identity rationale. Do not expand into neighbouring functions. Each function receives one window of at most 50 results on each of OpenAlex and Europe PMC.
4. **Supplementary blind search:** after the principal searches provisionally finish, run all eleven fixed families on both sources, at most 40 results per window. The families are coverage checks, not an exhaustive list of eligible functions. Screen every return; no second page or quota transfer.

Retain full-text attempts for screen-in, uncertain and title-retained abstract-unavailable papers. Preserve every search encounter, deduplicate logical works and reuse immutable completed snapshots. Newly discovered eligible papers/functions reopen the appropriate searches. The run ends only when all required searches and retrieval/evaluation obligations are resolved under the protocol, including explicit missing-text states.

Before a paper is semantically screened out, check the nonexclusive rescue routes in `PROTOCOL.md` §6.2: scalar-unclear three-gate cases, structured same-Y dissociations, response/rapid-chase priming, and domain coverage. Use the source-bound `tools/semantic_rescue.py` instrument to preserve triggered flags and audit demotions. A route flag schedules review; it never itself makes A/E/F or inclusion/exclusion true. Keep the exact abstract text/hash used for screening. Missing abstracts and ambiguous science remain visible.

## From full text to a comparison

| Situation | Additional work needed |
|---|---|
| Ordinary comparison with direct focal evidence | Record the seven judgments, located results and scoped constraint. Use the structured writer for IDs and history. |
| One function expressed through several conditions | Explain why these jointly measure one functional mapping; retain component evidence without substituting it for the whole function. |
| A premise borrowed from another experiment, supplement or source | Create direct evidence links and a premise-specific bundle. Justify transfer. A missing required result cannot support the conclusion. |
| Awareness comparison combines experiments | Locate the actual joint test, identify contributors and justify design/measure/population compatibility. Separate significance labels are not a joint test. |
| Reviewer calculates a statistic | Preserve located inputs, reproducible calculation and assumptions; do not invent unavailable covariance. |
| Null-oriented comparison | Distinguish an informative nondetection from region-based equivalence and model-relative Bayesian null support. |
| Reused full text or source fact | Use the verified version/hash and factual cache; reassess its scientific relevance to each comparison. |

The detailed reference rules and schema remain available for these branches. Automation handles identifiers, serialization, integrity and output membership; scientific judgments remain source-based.

## Outcomes and outputs

**Use without awareness** requires positive evidence for the exact whole function under adequately supported unawareness. It does not require matching an aware condition. It also does not establish equal performance across awareness conditions or that awareness adds nothing. Capacity-only studies remain supplementary. A capacity within a verified comparable human awareness design can enter the main profile when comparison-wide A is strong, with the signal-control limitation explicit. A separately supported local capacity with an uncertain higher arm remains in ordinary capacity/supplementary exports and can earn its process search.

**Awareness-linked change** requires a comparable contrast, adequate awareness evidence, a meaningful function, relevant signal control and an interpretable direct focal or compatible joint test. Weak control leaves a descriptive difference visible as unresolved for the stronger interpretation.

**No detectable difference** is weaker than **equivalence within a justified region** or **Bayesian support for a specified null relative to an alternative**. Stronger awareness interpretations require the corresponding design controls. None demonstrates preserved capacity without positive evidence that the function operates.

Outputs are mechanically derived from audited records and can overlap. Qualitative ordering states why evidence is stronger or weaker; no numerical score or reviewer majority vote substitutes for source evidence. Several rows from one experiment are not independent replications. Supporting, suggestive, complementary and mixed findings remain visible.

As part of normal full-text work, a separate checker verifies the decisive source passages under `AGENTS.md` §7 before a strong awareness-linked constraint or supported unaware capacity is exported. Its independent source interpretation precedes access to proposed labels, tiers or expected human decisions. Existing reasons distinguish exact X from awareness-measured content, conservative function from readout/mechanism, the actual functional test and its timing/scope, same-Y E where applicable, and statistics/limitations. The check is bound to current comparisons and sources. An unresolved check requires an explicit scientific revision before strong export; useful supplementary evidence remains visible. This is the existing execution check, not another scientific threshold, table or full-paper read.

The default source-linked handoff is a `profile-prospect`: the selected comparison and measured outcome, decisive evidence, potential contribution, limitation and concrete human question, followed by a tentative primary/support/undetermined role. Located same-function awareness arms can justify a prospect even without a direct difference test; the missing test remains explicit and no difference or equivalence is claimed. A source-grounded plausible awareness–function relation may remain unresolved. Known unaware-only studies or nonfunctional outcomes belong in context for this queue while retaining their scientific and discovery roles. Optional certified primary/support recommendations keep the stronger checks in `PROTOCOL.md` §12; those checks do not gate unfinished prospects.

The coordinator must update and deliver provisional prospect guides at validated checkpoints, accounting for newly assessed candidates and carrying earlier entries forward or recording a reasoned disposition. Continue the frozen searches autonomously. A final guide still requires the unchanged scientific completion and stopping conditions. Human acceptance remains pending until an explicit decision; tentative roles and model agreement cannot add to the human-vetted list. Prior explicit rejections remain visible and outside the first-read queue unless the user explicitly reopens them. Use `docs/run_quickstart.md` for delivery and `docs/postrun_handoff.md` for the fields.

When a result seems wrong, first distinguish a source, claim-scope or state error from genuine policy ambiguity. Correct the former under the existing rules; do not redesign the science merely to settle a tentative role.

They do not inspect one another's searches or judgments. After completion, union discoveries and adjudicate disagreements against source evidence, preserving original judgments and unresolved cases.
