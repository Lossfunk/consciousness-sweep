# Documentation and readiness disposition

The method is **0.9.13**; execution readiness is determined by `RELEASE_READINESS.json`. `PROTOCOL.md` and `AGENTS.md` remain authoritative for its scientific and execution rules. Source-bound abstracts, arm-wise awareness provenance, nonexcluding rescue/demotion audits and source-first scientific checks remain. The default deliverable is an argued prospect queue for human vetting. Scientific classifications and optional verified evidence recommendations retain their existing standards. Prior revisions remain historical provenance; they do not establish readiness of this revision.

The short workflow and run quickstart cover source assessment, checkpoint prospect delivery, scientific completion and explicit human decisions. Existing reasons separate X, awareness-measured content, conservative function, readout, actual test and scope. A prospect states its possible contribution, decisive evidence, limitation and human-vetting question. Tentative primary/support placement does not determine inclusion. Known absence of the relevant awareness comparison cannot be concealed as uncertainty; plausible unresolved comparisons remain visible with a specific question. This handoff does not restrict scientific capacity exports, earned searches or discovery. Storage codes and editorial tier labels add no scientific requirement.

Unrestricted dates, preprints and the complete manuscript-bibliography default are settled. A separately commissioned and newly frozen scope may use explicit author-vetted primary/support identities, with neutral provenance rather than expected judgments. Broad anchors and all eleven supplementary families remain required; they provide bounded coverage checks, not an exhaustive function taxonomy. Same-function terminology outside the triggering paper requires identity and provenance.

Remaining limitations/actions:

1. **Retrieval calibration is limited.** The historical check supports vocabulary quality, not measured recall or verified empirical validation of every exact final rendering. The preparation provenance correction preserves source echoes and original responses; it does not reconstruct missing request timestamps or create a compliant run retroactively.
2. **Scientific and recommendation judgments remain source-based.** Structural/schema/test success cannot establish future paper-level correctness or prove that a readout identifies a function. Prospect relevance is assessed from the actual comparison and sources; strong scientific promotions retain independent checking. Plausible unresolved prospects are not withheld merely because final evidential strength is unsettled. No expected paper classifications enter shipped instructions.
3. **Readiness is bound to the final inputs and stated checks.** Acceptance receipts state their exact scope. Tests of route preservation and prospect behavior are not measurements of semantic accuracy, unfamiliar-paper recall or full-sweep throughput. Existing source/claim/state mistakes are repaired locally unless they expose a real policy ambiguity. Human acceptance and primary/support consensus are not scientific completion obligations.

Historical development artifacts and diagnostics are excluded from independent-run handoffs. No historical run was migrated or consolidated. The manual semantic-review guide and structural crosswalk remain applicable.
