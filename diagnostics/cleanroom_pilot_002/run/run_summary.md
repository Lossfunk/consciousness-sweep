## Interpretation

The strongest supplied-source constraints are qualitative reorganizations of the same function: human cue use in Ben-Haim, the visible/unseen temporal crossover in Lin and Murray, matched-performance metacognitive divergence in Persaud, and threshold-linked nonmonotonic distractor interference in Tsushima. Lamy supplies repeated positive evidence for attentional capture under reported unawareness but cannot support an awareness-effect claim because E is only physical. Keizer supplies tentative unaware binding evidence, but the statistical fragility blocks the constraint-ready route. The animal rows in Ben-Haim are deliberately conservative because the supplied evidence does not establish A in those animals.

No row was labeled equivalence-supported, and no nonsignificant contrast was upgraded to invariance.
