## Exact diagnostic reasons

### Tsushima

The competing scalar evidence account predicts that stronger first-order motion evidence should produce at least as much distractor interference as threshold-region motion. Instead, the interference is non-monotonic, disappears at stronger visible coherence, and moves from about 5% to about 30% coherence when reduced contrast moves the perceptual threshold. Those results concern the same Y—regulation of motion-distractor influence on RSVP—and require the Exp. 1/2 bundle. This preserves the strong awareness-linked constraint. The threshold-region result demonstrates distractor interference, not successful regulation/control of that interference: the named focal capacity is therefore `not-supported` and no unaware-capacity row is emitted for this claim. Dedicated threshold assessment makes the failed indirect-over-direct sensitivity test supplementary, not inferentially constitutive. The existing Experiment 5 A-transfer-risk diagnostic remains unresolved and is not needed for this focal claim.

### Lin and Murray

The fixed-versus-varying location manipulation asks whether cue awareness changes the way exogenous spatial information is regulated, not whether two unrelated functions differ. The structured location-variation pattern contradicts a uniform weaker-evidence account and retains the conservative attention-regulation Y. Positive invisible cue facilitation demonstrates that attentional influence survives, but it does not positively demonstrate the named regulatory/inhibitory modulation. The focal capacity is therefore `not-supported`; the awareness-linked route is unchanged. The exact visibility-transfer and residual-information limitations remain recorded through the bundle rather than by promoting every control experiment to a candidate comparison.

### Lamy

The focal Y is coherent because cue relevance/relation and target location are explicit components of one task-dependent spatial mapping, and the critical statistic is the awareness × cue relation × location interaction. In Exps. 1-2, relevant-color cues capture attention when aware and unaware, whereas an irrelevant/different-color same-location cost appears when aware and is absent or near zero when unaware. The simple scalar account predicts that lower first-order cue evidence should merely attenuate every component of this same spatial relation. Exp. 3 contradicts that prediction: same-color capture is comparable across awareness states, while the different-color spatial effect is not uniformly smaller under unawareness and may be larger. The complete bundle therefore supports `E-diagnostic`; Exp. 3 is support-only unless independently coded for another qualifying X-to-Y question. The specifically named component capacity—task-relevant cue-driven attentional capture/spatial influence—is positively present under unawareness. The awareness-linked irrelevant-cue mismatch/update-like consequence is not claimed to survive without awareness.

### Ben-Haim

Exp. 7 supplies the focal qualitative change: low/no awareness supports automatic cue following, while awareness permits strategic application of the instructed opposite-location rule. A simple evidence-strength account predicts a weaker version of the same cue-use direction, not reversal of the cue-to-choice mapping. The named component capacity is automatic cue-guided behavioral bias and is supported. Strategic opposite-location rule use is not supported under unawareness; the automatic component is not evidence that strategy survives. The late-experiment bundle supplies rule-comprehension and robustness support without requiring every experiment in the paper to qualify.

### Persaud

First-order discrimination was titrated/matched before comparing metacognitive calibration. Thus E is based on matching, not on a cross-process dissociation. The reported blind-field wagering-accuracy correlation is r=.30, but the supplied article does not report an adequate direct positive test of that correlation against zero, so the named blind-field capacity—metacognitive calibration of confidence to discrimination performance—is `unclear`. The between-field correlation difference still supports the awareness-linked result, but generality remains limited by the single blindsight participant and the special neuropsychological population.

### Keizer

The effectively masked condition has a positive binding cost in the primary awareness-qualified analysis, directly instantiating the named capacity—orientation-location binding and retrieval—so `unaware_capacity = supported`. The individually calibrated masking, stronger masks used in the binding task, post-task visibility evaluation, and exclusion/retest procedure provide an independently justified awareness classification; Meyen is therefore supplementary. The more inclusive reanalysis weakens the binding result, so the capacity constraint carries a material robustness limitation rather than being described as unqualified.

The mask × binding interaction was directly tested for the principal response-time result, accuracy, and the inclusive reanalysis without a detected awareness modulation. That warrants the descriptive category `awareness_effect = no-detectable-difference`. It does not warrant an awareness-difference constraint, and it does not establish sameness. No equivalence margin, supportive Bayes factor, or informative confidence region was reported, so `equivalence-supported` is prohibited.

## Remaining genuine weaknesses

- Tsushima Experiment 5 lacks reported same-participant visibility results and remains `A-transfer-risk`; the focal regression instead rests on the adequately reported threshold-linked behavioral bundle.
- Lin's objective-invisibility transfer and indirect/direct sensitivity relation retain residual uncertainty even though they do not supply the E-diagnostic inference.
- Lamy's E decision requires the complete multi-experiment bundle and a conservative common Y; it would fail if the component effects were recast as unrelated processes.
- Ben-Haim's strongest result is late and experiment-specific; the whole paper should not be generalized into a single qualifying claim.
- Persaud is a single-case blindsight study, limiting population generality.
- Keizer's unaware binding effect is sensitive to the alternative inclusive analysis, and its negative interaction evidence is not positive evidence of equivalence.
