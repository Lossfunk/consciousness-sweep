# Clean-room pilot 003 adjudication

## Scope and standard

This diagnostic-only adjudication compares the byte-preserved pilot-003 run in `diagnostics/cleanroom_pilot_003/run/` against the hand-audited anchors, pilots 001/002, accepted gold corrections, the draft-0.8.3 revision diagnostics, and the locally supplied article fixture. It does not alter the archived run or registered-facing scientific results.

Classes: **A CLEAN PASS**, **B SOURCE-FIXTURE LIMITATION**, **C GOLD-STANDARD CORRECTION**, **D ORDINARY MODEL VARIANCE**, **E GENERIC METHOD UNDERSPECIFICATION**, **F METHOD OVER-PERMISSIVENESS**, **G PROVENANCE / EXECUTION FAILURE**.

## Pilot-wide finding

Pilot 003 fixed the prior functional-structure, focal-capacity identity, and weak-E descriptive-audit defects. It exposed two narrower defects. First, an unmeasured trialwise-attention possibility was allowed to veto E while an explicit same-article quantitative-alternative control was not evaluated. Second, a described awareness procedure and broad figure citation were accepted as if they supplied an experiment/sample-specific awareness result. The latter silently propagated threshold results into rows whose critical result was not reported.

## Structural implication after draft-0.8.4

- The factorial focal relation is found before decomposition.
- Unanchored attention/arousal hypotheses cannot act as E vetoes.
- Same-article controls must be scanned and classification-critical candidates evaluated before final unmatched E.
- Early design-/behavior-grounded task-policy confounds remain gating; later fixed-policy cases are not defeated by generic speculation.
- Threshold-shift E remains viable.
- Strong A requires an actual procedure-matched result and source; separate results require explicit transfer.
- The fMRI/sample-transfer case remains cautious because its critical visibility result is absent.
- Keizer retains descriptive no-detectable without equivalence and with capacity fragility visible.
- Persaud retains the awareness-linked difference while blind-field capacity remains unclear.
