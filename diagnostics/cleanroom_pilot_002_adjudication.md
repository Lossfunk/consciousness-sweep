# Clean-room pilot 002 adjudication

## Scope

This is diagnostic calibration material. It is excluded from registered-facing inputs and future independent-run fixtures. The byte-preserved source run is archived at `diagnostics/cleanroom_pilot_002/`; it used commit `bdb762240b9df98b921e8813b566fc9bb1be5100`, draft-0.8.2, six supplied main articles, and the same-article Tsushima supplement. It had no external auxiliary literature, network retrieval, diagnostics, tests, gold material, or manuscript material.

The adjudication compares the durable pilot-002 records against the current hand-audited anchor standard, pilot 001, and accepted pilot-001 corrections. It does not rescreen literature and does not rewrite the archived classifications.

Classes:

- **A — CLEAN PASS**
- **B — SOURCE-FIXTURE LIMITATION**
- **C — GOLD-STANDARD CORRECTION**
- **D — ORDINARY MODEL VARIANCE**
- **E — GENERIC METHOD UNDERSPECIFICATION**
- **F — METHOD OVER-PERMISSIVENESS**

## Pilot-wide result

Pilot 002 confirms four generic defects in draft-0.8.2. First, effect and mechanism labels could still justify scalarization without an operational functional-identity test. Second, a comparison could claim an unaware capacity different from its focal Y. Third, weak E could suppress the durable record of a direct same-Y aware/unaware test. Fourth, a structured reversal could receive `E-diagnostic` without resolving a separately design-motivated task-policy state that could itself determine Y.

The pilot also confirms important non-regressions. Strong A was recoverable from same-article supplementary evidence without importing external literature. Persaud's corrected result was reproduced. No paper was upgraded from a nonsignificant comparison to equivalence. Threshold-shift/factorial E reasoning remained representable. Evidence bundles were explicit and premise-specific even where the new contrast-integrity member was missing.

## Diagnostic conclusions by class

- **A — clean pass:** source-complete behavioral A handling for Tsushima; Persaud's corrected awareness/capacity separation; meaningful-versus-BOLD F distinctions; non-equivalence restraint; most X/Y and statistical extraction; explicit evidence provenance.
- **B — source-fixture limitation:** unavailable external monkey validation in Ben-Haim. The Tsushima fMRI sample limitation is sample-specific rather than a missing supplied source.
- **C — gold-standard correction:** Persaud's blind-field capacity remains `unclear`; pilot 002 correctly follows the accepted correction.
- **D — ordinary model variance:** whether Lin's immediate and later effects are best represented as one spatiotemporal mapping or distinct temporal functions, subject now to explicit positive functional-identity justification.
- **E — generic method underspecification:** Lamy scalarization without a focal operational mapping; Keizer's missing descriptive direct-comparison audit; the previously implicit functional-identity basis.
- **F — method over-permissiveness:** early Ben-Haim E-diagnostic without task-policy contrast integrity; Tsushima and Lin parent capacity claims supported by different component consequences; the general capacity-target loophole.

## Structural implications of draft-0.8.3

This is a diagnostic re-evaluation, not pilot 003 and not a new scientific run:

- Lamy-like decompositions must first instantiate and audit a focal operational mapping when the six functional-identity questions support one; linked components may coexist.
- A Tsushima-like control/regulation row cannot claim capacity from observed interference. A separate interference row is optional and must earn F and capacity independently.
- A Keizer-like direct same-Y interaction receives a descriptive audit despite weak E; no-detectable remains descriptive and equivalence still needs positive evidence.
- Persaud's awareness-linked metacognitive difference remains, while blind-field positive capacity remains unclear.
- Early Ben-Haim-like reversals must name and resolve any finite, design-motivated policy/knowledge alternative before `E-diagnostic`.
- Later fixed-task, fixed-policy within-regime comparisons are not rejected merely because sensory evidence varies.
- Threshold-linked nonmonotonicity plus an experimentally shifted threshold remains eligible for ordinary strict E-diagnostic analysis when no separate task-state cochange is identified.
