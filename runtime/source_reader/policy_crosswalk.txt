Authority: development/current/PROTOCOL.md, version 0.9.13. These instructions
are a derived reading aid, not an amendment to scientific eligibility. The full
protocol is not reproduced in the model prompt. Runtime bindings fingerprint
this aid and the operative authorities; identifiers, source hashes, attempt

Scientific instructions -> controlling PROTOCOL sections

Source-grounded prospects, supported claim before tentative role, useful bounded
associations rather than independent causation by awareness, and result-neutral
review -> §§1, 12. The practical queue serves human vetting. A provisional
prospect is not a certified primary/support recommendation.

Aligned existing X, awareness of that content and downstream Y; internally
generated content distinguished from absent content; qualitative reports,
graded/probability regimes, actual observations rather than procedure labels,
objective invisibility distinguished from above-chance performance, timing and
claim-scoped transfer -> §§1, 2.1, 2.4, 12. Changing X does not automatically
transfer an old awareness measure. Awareness of constituents does not by itself
establish awareness of their composed relational content. This does not require
a separate identification response for every property or a unique neural format.

Same-item reports can support a plausible unresolved awareness interpretation
while retaining retention/report confounds. Postlearning amount/correctness of
knowledge requires a positive reason to measure awareness of that same content.
Neither a universal PAS ban nor automatic report-based admission follows ->
§§1, 2.1, 12.

Conservative function distinguished from readout and unique mechanism; direct
registration of X distinguished from downstream use; later-target discrimination
as a possible outcome of earlier-X use; coherent functional mapping distinguished
from a positive component -> §§2.4–2.6, 3.1. Each materially distinct proposed
X→Y mapping earns its own claim-scoped evidence argument. A new label does not
repair a failed premise, but an independently useful narrower claim can survive.

Comparability, achieved relevant titration versus nonsignificance, physical versus
internal matching, diagnostic same-Y patterns, signed amplification and bounded
alternative-control scan -> §§2.2–2.3. Ordinary stronger/longer/unmasked input
producing a larger effect is not made informative merely by listing the signal
confound. A prospect's contribution argument identifies located evidence for
what the comparison adds. This is not a universal matching or primary-level E
gate: physical-only matching with internal uncertainty, diagnostic patterns across
strength and genuine unresolved aligned comparisons can remain useful prospects.

Strongest relevant supplied supplementary result, source/version compatibility,
required versus strengthening evidence, and direct justified transfer rather
than silently inherited conclusions -> §§2.1, 7.1–7.2, 8. A main contrast's
limitation does not excuse overlooking narrower within-setting or nondifference
analyses. Missing a strengthening donor does not invalidate an independently
supported narrow claim. Known absent evidence differs from uncertain evidence.

Actual direct/joint contrast distinguished from separate significance labels;
positive whole-function capacity distinguished from equivalence; nondetection
not equated with zero effect, incapacity or awareness necessity; material
statistical and same-Y conflicting evidence retained -> §§3–5, 7.2, 8.
Uncertainty and concern matter through their bearing on the claim, not automatic
exclusion due to one patient, exploratory analysis or a significance crossing.

Holistic source-grounded contribution, known absent/incompatible comparisons or
nonfunctional outcomes kept in context, and concrete human judgment rather than
a promise that rereading settles every causal alternative -> §§1, 12. A known
unaware-only capacity can retain supplementary scientific value without becoming
an awareness-comparison prospect. No paper names, expected verdicts, numerical
scientific scores or keyword classification gates appear in this contract.

Execution boundary

This short model output is deliberately not the native physical-experiment,
comparison, awareness-regime, statistical-test or evidence-bundle ledger. It
contains a few materially distinct claim trails and decisive located evidence.
Shared methods and limitations need not be repeated; incidental statistics and
duplicate demonstrations need not be enumerated. Source coverage is unchanged.
Native audits, IDs, physical group enumeration and claim/bundle records remain
subject to AGENTS §§5–7, 9–12 and PROTOCOL §8 when that separate certification
workflow is invoked. No native support-only comparison row is invented here.

schema.py validates strict structure, source-identity status consistency, exact
asset/unit pair references and unread-unit exceptions. The same page label in
article and supplement is not the same source unit. A complete assessment must
include at least one source-located claim/decision trail, including for context;
an incomplete or identity-unresolved assessment may have none. Complete attests
that all supplied extracted units were read, not that every scientific premise is resolved
or that unseen pixels were inspected. Missing external texts and other extraction
limitations are reported separately. This self-report does not mechanically prove
reading quality; source checking remains necessary. The validator neither
interprets prose nor certifies a scientific disposition, source transfer,
statistical inference or primary/support tier.

Incomplete or source-identity-unresolved assessments remain visibly incomplete;
transport validation is not scientific acceptance. A staged prospect and even a
completed bounded commissioning task do not certify native strong exports,
discovery completeness, title/abstract recall, registered-sweep stopping rules
or human acceptance. These remain separate explicit statuses and obligations.
